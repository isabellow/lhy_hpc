HPC AMB154, empty checks

Each row is one cell and each column a time bin (20 ms) relative to check onset (-1 to 1 s) and offset (-1 to 1 s); dashed line at 0. Empty checks: site interactions with no seed change lasting <= 1.5 s, at a site the seed ledger scores as empty.
Activity: each cell's instantaneous firing rate over the whole session was divided by its session SD (+0.01) and a running-average baseline (window 30) was subtracted (pop_normalize), so units are SDs from baseline; this trace was averaged across checks and Gaussian-smoothed (sigma 20 ms). Colour limits -0.5 to 0.5 SD (red above, blue below baseline).
Per cell, checks were excluded where the unit's amplitude in a 1-min window around the check was below 0.7x its session average.
A cell is drawn only with >= 3 usable checks. Cells: all good units, session firing rate > 0.1 Hz, in the projection nucleus (on or beside stim-responsive channels); sessions without stim data excluded.

Sorting: ordered by the time of each cell's peak (the whole window, onset-aligned), earliest on top.
Cross-validated: each session's checks were split in half (alternating checks in time); half 0 set the order and the heatmap shows only half 1.

174 cells, 5 sessions, 1 bird.
Checks: 9056 checks, 8864 of them empty; 1 too close to the session edge; 4430 in the plotted half, median 934.5 per cell after the drift cut.
Median check duration 0.22 s.
